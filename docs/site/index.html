SONAVA

The player for the music you already control. Local files, your own server, your subscriptions — one place, no tracking.

What it does

  • Plays your files, your Subsonic/Navidrome server, WebDAV drives and your Apple Music subscription — together.
  • True gapless, EBU R128 levelling, a 10-band EQ that works on streams, and correction profiles for your exact headphones.
  • Crate Mix: your queue ordered like a DJ's crate, transitions on the beat.
  • Your listening biography: import years of history, see what you truly own, and get an honest year recap — counted on your phone.
  • Private by design: no accounts, no analytics, no ads.
