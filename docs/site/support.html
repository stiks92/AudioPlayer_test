Common questions

My server won't connect. Check the full address with scheme (https:// or http:// for LAN), username and password. Navidrome, Airsonic and any OpenSubsonic server work.

Yandex.Disk / Mail.ru asks for a password repeatedly. Cloud drives need an app password, not your account password — create one in the service's security settings.

Apple Music says "no subscription". Playback of the full catalogue needs an active Apple Music subscription on this Apple ID.

Эквалайзер не действует на Apple Music. Apple Music играет в защищённом плеере Apple — обработка звука к нему не применяется; на файлах, серверах и стримах всё работает.
